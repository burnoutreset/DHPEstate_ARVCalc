Foreclosure Resources

Auction platforms · County sheriff sales · Circuit clerk case searches

Weekly Routine (5 min, every Thursday morning)
  1. Open the McHenry sheriff foreclosure PDF — Thursday sale list
  2. Open the Lake County Judicial Sales calendar
  3. Open the Kane County sheriff foreclosure PDF
  4. Quick scan for properties in your target zips (Crystal Lake, Cary, Lake in the Hills, Algonquin, etc.)
  5. Note any addresses worth research — observe, don't bid yet
Monthly Deep-Dive (15-20 min)
  1. Open each county's Circuit Clerk case search
  2. Search Chancery / Foreclosure cases filed in the last 30 days
  3. Pull case files for properties in your target zips
  4. Drive by the most promising 2-3 properties
  5. Run ARV in this calculator on the ones worth pursuing
Year-1 caution: Don't bid at sheriff's sale your first year. Attend as an observer. Sheriff sales are cash-only, no interior inspection, may include occupied property with eviction needed. Start with Hubzu/Xome/HUD REO instead — those let you inspect and finance normally.
The high-quality channel everyone misses: The Circuit Clerk pre-foreclosure case search shows properties 6–18 months before they go to auction. That's when the homeowner still has title and you can approach with a direct cash offer. Send 1 professional letter via USPS — never aggressive, never urgent. Follow up once at 60 days, then never again. Low response rate but high-quality leads.

REO / Auction Platforms You're On

When to use: Daily passive monitoring. These send email alerts. Bank-owned REOs — you can inspect first, finance normally, close like a regular transaction. Lower risk than courthouse-step sheriff sales.
HUD

HUD Home Store Login

hudhomestore.gov

FHA-insured foreclosures. Owner-occupant priority window first 15-30 days — investors locked out during that window. As a licensed IL agent, look into becoming a HUD-registered broker to submit your own offers and keep the 3% commission.

HZ

Hubzu Login

hubzu.com

REO from Ocwen and other servicers. Auction-style with reserves; banks often counter even after you "win." Watch for 5% buyer's premium on top of your bid — always model into MAO.

↗

Xome Login

xome.com

Mr. Cooper's REO disposition platform. Different inventory than Hubzu — worth checking weekly. Similar fee structure to Hubzu.
